## 2. E2E track (platform-e2e; FEATURES.yaml is the e2e-author's job)

New feature `platform-e2e/tests/e2e/features/recommendations/recsys_auto_train.feature`, tags `@recsys @recommendations @destructive`. Determinism (D13): every scenario starts with a reset step (delete the `recsys-*` registered models, flush `recs:v1:*`, drop generation collections), then seeds explicitly: registry champions through `seed`, a prior generation `g1` published, the feature snapshots present. Pass and fail are driven by seeding a weak or a strong champion. The fixture is sized so that ALS, GBDT and two-tower all pass against weak champions. Stack subset: redis, qdrant, postgres-mlflow, minio, mlflow, team-ai (+ gateway as existing scenarios need), then the `jobs` profile recsys job.
