## Requirements

### Requirement: Browse available vouchers

The system SHALL present a Vouchers Hub at `/vouchers` that lists the vouchers
currently available to a visitor, each with a way to save it.

#### Scenario: Visitor browses available vouchers

- **WHEN** a visitor opens the vouchers page
- **THEN** the Vouchers Hub is displayed with at least one available voucher
