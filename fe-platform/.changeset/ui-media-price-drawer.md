`@fe-platform/ui` adds `PriceTag` with `formatPrice`, `Image` (fixed aspect box, lazy, error fallback, `radius`), `Avatar` with `initials`, and a Radix `Drawer` side panel. Tokens add the `priceTag`, `image`, `avatar` and `drawer` class maps, spacing steps 14 and 20, and the `4/3`, `3/4` and `2/1` aspect ratios in the preset.
