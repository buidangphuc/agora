Adds the `action-danger` and `action-danger-hover` semantic colours (light and dark, contrast-gated against `text-inverse`), the `danger-darker` primitive, and class-map entries for Button `danger`, `xs`, `fullWidth`, `icon`, `spinner` and the Card parts. `card.base` no longer carries padding; `card.padded` does.
